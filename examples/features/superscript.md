# Superscript

Inline superscript uses `^...^` and renders as true OOXML superscript (real vertical
alignment), not Unicode superscript glyphs.

## Math and units

The area of a circle is A = πr^2^, and Einstein's relation is E = mc^2^.

Powers of ten scale fast: 10^3^ is a thousand, 10^6^ is a million, and 2^10^ = 1024.

## Footnote-style ordinals

The 1^st^, 2^nd^, and 3^rd^ entries are marked with superscript ordinal suffixes.

## Combined with emphasis

A **bold x^2^** term and an *italic a^n^* term keep their emphasis and superscript together.
